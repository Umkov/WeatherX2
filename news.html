CYCLONE GEZANI MAKES LANDFALL ON MADAGASCAR

Cyclone Gezani has made landfall near Toamasina, Madagascar with sustained winds of 140 miles per hour and a central pressure of 956 millibars per our analysis. It is expected to reemerge into the Mozambique Channel where it could reintensify and affect southern Mozambique. Residents in the affected area should stay sheltered until the cyclone passes.
